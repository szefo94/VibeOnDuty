## Verdict

The contract is implemented faithfully in the session tool: arms, order, both rules in the cell
form, validity before timing, binding, calibration scope, the registry and its history check, the
cap and the verdict all match the text, the runner follows the seven steps, and an in-memory run of
the real runner with historical samples produced reports that the real frozen command and the
`verify` function accept. The CR-01 and CR-03 fixes hold on both routes and now have controls with
teeth. One thing does not hold, and it is the reason for the status: the plan 09 predicate decides
from the registry's `closed` events alone and never checks a counted session against its reports.
With six hand-written registry events and a four-field stub file, the real `requirePlan09` accepted
the existing single-arm plan 07-13 candidate (the report the frozen gate failed, 40 statistics over)
as a paired qualification. No capture is needed for that and every control stays green. The fix is
small and is described in I-01. It has to be made now: once the calibration is registered, any
change to these three files ends the paired route for the identity.
