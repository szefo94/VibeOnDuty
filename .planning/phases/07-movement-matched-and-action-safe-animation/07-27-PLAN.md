## Design, decided from source

Frozen budget facts (`tools/art/animation-budget-v2.mjs` line 21): `src/builders/enemyGLTF.js` and `src/builders/enemyAnimations.js` are two of the five `MUTABLE_RUNTIME` files. Every other existing file under `src/` is a frozen reference identity. `src/builders/enemyGLTF.test.js` asserts `findMotionClip(..., 'walk')` is `vod7_walk_forward` and is frozen. So the repair must not change what `findMotionClip` and `getMotionDefinition` return; it adds a second lookup used only where live characters are built.

- New in `src/builders/enemyGLTF.js`: an exported frozen table `LIVE_INHERITED_ROUTE` and an exported `findLiveClip(animations, key, weapon)`. For a key in the table it resolves through `ALIASES` only (the inherited and `rifle_` names); for every other key it returns exactly what `findMotionClip` returns.
- Route table (key to `ALIASES` key):
  - legacy keys resolve to themselves: `idle`, `walk`, `walk_back`, `run`, `run_back`, `strafe_l`, `strafe_r`, `crouch`, `crouch_walk`, `jump_start`, `jump_loop`, `jump_land`, `death`, `hit`;
  - `walk_forward` to `walk`, `walk_left` to `strafe_l`, `walk_right` to `strafe_r`; `run_forward` to `run`, `run_left` to `strafe_l`, `run_right` to `strafe_r`; `sprint` to `run`;
  - `crouch_idle` to `crouch`; `crouch_forward`, `crouch_back`, `crouch_left`, `crouch_right` to `crouch_walk` (the inherited set has one crouch walk; this is what `PRESENTATION_FALLBACK` already says);
  - `death_front`, `death_back`, `death_left`, `death_right` to `death` (one inherited death; direction is lost);
  - `stop`, `reversal`, `turn_left`, `turn_right` to `idle` (no inherited equivalent; the feet stay planted in the idle pose);
  - `damage` to `hit`.
- Not in the table, so unchanged: `<weapon>_ready`, `<weapon>_ads`, `<weapon>_fire`, `<weapon>_reload`, `attack`, `shoot`, `reload`, `melee_cross`, `melee_jab`, `throw`, `punch_cross`, `punch_jab`, `nade`, `roll`, `dance`, and every preview action. Reason: the owner's list names none of them, first person (which copies the upper body) was judged "mostly okay", and they are weapon handling.
- Call sites that switch to `findLiveClip`: the action loops of `buildEnemyMesh`, `buildPlayerMesh` and `createReviewCharacter`, and `_sharedHitAdditiveClip` (both `hit` and its `idle` reference, as before e08e0ac). `normaliseClipQuatSigns` keeps its present reference clip. `prepareEnemyClips` additionally aligns the live jump chain (`rifle_Jump_Start` to `rifle_Jump_Loop` to `rifle_Jump_Land`) the way it was aligned before e08e0ac; the existing alignment of the authored chain stays.
- In `src/builders/enemyAnimations.js`: the idle lower layer. `animationPresentation.js` (frozen) names the standing gait `<weapon>_ready`; `presentationAction` therefore takes the lower mask of a `_ready` key from `e.actions.idle` and the upper mask from the `_ready` action as today. The source cache must key by layer side as well as key. Nothing else in layer selection changes.
- The inertia fix, same file: bones written by `_tickInertia` are restored to the value the mixer left before the next `mixer.update`, in the same place and order the presentation correction is restored (restore, mixer, correction, inertia), for every caller of `_tickInertia`. No allocation is added per bone and frame.
