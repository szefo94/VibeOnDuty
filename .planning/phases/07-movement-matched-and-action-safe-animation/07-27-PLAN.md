## Design, decided from measurement

All figures below were measured on the real skeleton from `public/models/enemy.glb` with the `rifle_` clips composed, 12 phases per clip; character forward is +Z; "weapon forward" is the +Y axis of `hand_r` in the character frame; knee offset is the distance of the knee from the hip to ankle line along +Z. The measuring scripts are throwaway; the tests of this plan re-measure everything.

Why not a mask swap. Inherited lower mask under the authored ready upper mask: weapon yaw -40 degrees standing, -41 to -27 walking, -67 to -50 strafing; crouch pitch -18. Whole inherited clips: weapon yaw between -6.8 and +5.8 and pitch between -0.5 and +8.0 over all gaits, with `spine_03` itself at -16 to -56 degrees of yaw and 6 to 28 degrees of forward pitch (the mocap stance is bladed and leaning; the arms cancel it). Today's authored pose: weapon yaw 0, pitch 0, `spine_03` yaw within 0.7 degrees.

Why not the whole inherited clip when no weapon action runs. First person copies the arm bones of the same skeleton, was judged "mostly okay", and would change in every idle and moving frame; and each shot, reload or ADS would still have to put the authored upper body on a pelvis turned 40 degrees and a chest leaning 26, a visible pop at every action. It also changes the driver's selection, which the fixture comparison in `tests/animation-resources.spec.js` pins.

Chosen design: composed clips in the action map.

- `src/builders/enemyGLTF.js` gains an exported `buildLiveClips(scene, animations)` that returns a map of composed clips, built once after `prepareEnemyClips` and kept beside the template (not pushed into `gltf.animations`, so the prepared set and `findMotionClip` are unchanged), and an exported `findLiveClip(animations, key, weapon)` that returns the composed clip for a routed key and exactly `findMotionClip`'s result for every other key. The action loops of `buildEnemyMesh`, `buildPlayerMesh` and `createReviewCharacter` use `findLiveClip`.
- A composed clip is named `live_<action id>` (for example `live_walk_forward`, `live_crouch_idle`, `live_m4_ready`), has the duration of its inherited source and exactly these tracks:
  - lower, 10 tracks: `pelvis.position` and the quaternions of `pelvis`, `thigh_l`, `thigh_r`, `calf_l`, `calf_r`, `foot_l`, `foot_r`, `ball_l`, `ball_r`, keyed at the inherited clip's key times. Pelvis position, calf, foot and ball are the inherited values. The pelvis orientation is the authored clip's pelvis orientation (constant in every authored gait) with the inherited pelvis's deviation from its own clip mean applied on top, scaled by a sway factor. Each thigh is re-expressed so that its orientation in the character frame equals the inherited one under the new pelvis.
  - upper: every track of the authored clip of the same action id that the driver's `UPPER_TRACK` pattern matches, unchanged in value, with key times scaled to the composed duration.
- Sway factor: 0.25 for idle and the gaits (walk, run, sprint, and the idle legs used for stop, reversal and turns); 0 for the crouch and jump families, whose pre-e08e0ac `rifle_` clips held the chest fixed in the character frame.
- Route (action id, inherited source of the lower tracks, authored source of the upper tracks):
  - `<weapon>_ready` for each of the four weapons, and the legacy key `idle`: inherited idle (`attack`); authored ready of that weapon;
  - `walk_forward` and legacy `walk`: `walk`; `walk_back`: `walk_back`; `walk_left` and legacy `strafe_l`: `strafe_l`; `walk_right` and legacy `strafe_r`: `strafe_r`; `run_forward` and legacy `run`: `run`; `run_back`: `run_back`; `run_left`: `strafe_l`; `run_right`: `strafe_r`; `sprint`: `run`; each with its own authored upper;
  - `crouch_idle` and legacy `crouch`: `rifle_Crouch_Idle_Loop`; `crouch_forward`, `crouch_back`, `crouch_left`, `crouch_right` and legacy `crouch_walk`: `rifle_Crouch_Fwd_Loop` (the inherited set has one crouch walk);
  - `jump_start`, `jump_loop`, `jump_land`: `rifle_Jump_Start`, `rifle_Jump_Loop`, `rifle_Jump_Land`, with quaternion signs continuous across the two boundaries;
  - `stop`, `reversal`, `turn_left`, `turn_right`: inherited idle legs under their own authored upper;
  - `death`, `death_front`, `death_back`, `death_left`, `death_right`: the inherited death (`Death01`) whole, not composed; direction is lost;
  - `hit` and its idle reference inside `_sharedHitAdditiveClip`, and `damage`: the inherited `hit` and inherited idle whole, as before e08e0ac.
- Unchanged, resolved as today: `<weapon>_ads`, `_fire`, `_reload`, `attack`, `shoot`, `reload`, `melee_cross`, `melee_jab`, `throw`, `punch_cross`, `punch_jab`, `nade`, `roll`, `dance`, every preview action.
- `src/builders/enemyAnimations.js`: only the inertia fix. Bones written by `_tickInertia` are restored to the value the mixer left before the next `mixer.update`, in the order restore, mixer, correction, inertia, for every caller. Layer selection, masks, `UPPER_TRACK`, `PRESENTATION_FALLBACK` and source caching are not touched.

Guard limits, fixed here and not loosened after a result:
- Weapon forward yaw within 3.5 degrees and its peak-to-peak range over a cycle at most 6.3 degrees (the largest range inside one whole inherited clip, `run_back`); weapon forward pitch within 2.5 degrees of the authored ready's; `spine_03` yaw within 3.5 degrees, also with a fire or reload upper layer.
- Largest forward knee offset in the cycle at least 0.06 m for every in-scope state (inherited 0.099 and more, authored 0.015 and less). Crouch states also: smallest knee offset at least 0.15 m (measured 0.172) and pelvis never above 0.55 m.
- Lowest ankle or ball: at least -0.03 m for idle, gaits, crouch and the inherited death; at least -0.02 m for jump land; for jump start and jump loop, whose feet leave the root plane while the game lifts the root, at least -0.12 m, stated as an airborne bound and not as ground contact.
- Death: final pelvis at most 0.12 m. Damage: additive from the inherited `hit` has a largest bone rotation of at least 30 degrees and at least 5 times that of one built from `vod7_damage`.
- Track count: each composed clip has exactly 10 lower tracks (the authored clips have 9 or 10), so the mixer's per-layer track count stays where the paired timing gate's reference arm has it.
