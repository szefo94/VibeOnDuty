## Property, limits and where each number comes from

- Ink share: pixels of the outline ink colour (red 8, green 9, blue 11, as the reviewer counted) over all pixels of the frame. Before the repair began: 0.1 to 0.3 percent in every crouched frame of five runs. Limit: at most 1.0 percent in every frame of this plan's browser spec, standing and crouched, every pitch, still and turning. The margin is three times the largest earlier figure; the defect measures 20 to 48 percent.
- Own-body share: pixels that differ between the frame and the same frame rendered with the body view hidden (arms view and view weapon unchanged in both), over all pixels. Measured, not assumed, in task 1 for standing at every pitch. Limit for pitches above -0.3 rad: the crouched share is at most the standing share at the same pitch plus 0.5 percentage points (the standing case is clean and is the reference).
- The look-down exception, defined so that it can be measured (CONTEXT D-02, "torso and legs when looking down"): at pitches at or below -0.6 rad the body may fill any share of the frame provided that the ink limit holds, at least two of `calf_l`, `calf_r`, `foot_l`, `foot_r` of the body view are on screen with both coordinates inside 0.9, and the two Node conditions hold. Between -0.6 and -0.3 rad only the ink limit and the Node conditions apply.
- Node conditions, on the real rig and the real body-view meshes with their skinned triangles: the eye is inside no closed body-view mesh (odd number of surface crossings along any of three fixed rays), and the nearest point of any visible body-view triangle is at least 0.10 m from the eye. Basis: the camera's near plane is 0.05 m (`src/scene.js` line 17); standing is 0.22 m clear and clean; crouched is 0.042 to 0.060 m at `2f69dfe` and 0.000 to 0.005 m with the lean, which is inside the near plane, so the wall in front of the eye is clipped and the inside of the outline hull is drawn. 0.10 m is twice the near plane.
- No limit is changed after a measurement. If the property cannot be made to hold by changes inside `src/builders/firstPersonRig.js` (and, only for the fade rule below, `src/builders/weaponHandling.js`), stop and return to the orchestrator with the figures and the options.

## The pitch fade of the lean: kept

The fade (full lean up to +0.3 rad of aim, none from +0.8) was added only to protect the first-person body view. After B-01 the lean never reaches that view, so the fade protects nothing in first person. It is kept all the same: the owner previewed and accepted the lean with the fade (`07-36-OWNER-PREVIEW.md`, `lean: right`), removing it would change the third-person look the owner has seen and force the 6 to 13 degree range and its guard cases to be derived again, and nothing measured shows the un-faded look to be better. Consequences stated: a crouched character aiming up still straightens in third person; the 6 to 13 range stays as tested, although its first-person reason has gone; the guard cases of Amendment 1 in `crouchTorsoLean.test.js` stay, renamed in a comment for what they are (a count of torso vertices in view).
