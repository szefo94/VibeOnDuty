The inherited gait clips already have the pelvis at the authored horizontal position (0, -0.050), so the gaits are not touched; their shoulder sway of about 0.01 m comes from the quarter pelvis sway and gave contact error 0 in the debugger's measurement. Crouched head height is 1.12 to 1.13 m in every variant. Before e08e0ac the armed crouch had the head 0.085 m and the chest 0.184 m behind the root.

Two designs were compared.
- (A) Extend the view's shoulder fit to the fore and aft axis in `firstPersonRig.js`. It makes the first-person error 0 (debugger's trial) but leaves the third-person head and chest 0.23 m behind the simulated player, removes the small gait sway of the view shoulders unless bounded, and changes a second measured file and the look-down test's arms formula.
- (B) Hold the pelvis of the composed crouch and jump clips at the authored horizontal position and take only the height from the inherited clip. The shoulder shift becomes exactly 0, which is the shoulder placement the authored crouch had before the repair, so the first-person geometry is the pre-repair one for every weapon by construction; the third-person head and chest come back over the root; one mutable file changes.

Chosen: (B), with the ankles kept at the inherited positions and the legs re-solved to them by the existing two-bone solve. Shifting the ankles with the pelvis instead would keep today's leg shape but put the feet 0.23 m forward of where the inherited clip and the old game place them (front foot 0.45 m ahead of the root, outside the 0.35 m body radius) and slide them on every crouch entry. With the ankles kept, the feet stay planted where they are today and the pelvis moves over them; knees stay in front in every clip. Design (A) is not added: with (B) the shoulder shift is 0 and nothing is left for it to correct.

Rule, one for both families and not tuned per clip: in `buildLiveClips`, for every composed clip whose sway factor is 0 (the crouch and jump families), the pelvis position track keeps the inherited height and takes its two horizontal components from the authored clip of the same action id at each key; the leg re-solve then runs as before against the inherited ankle positions.

Axes, verified in the GLB and by measurement, not assumed: the pelvis is a child of `root`, which is rotated -90 degrees about X, so in the `pelvis.position` track the third component (local z) is the height, the first (local x) is lateral and the second (local y) is fore and aft. Rest translation of the pelvis node: (0, 0.0501, 0.9167). Composed standing (0, 0.0501, 0.8768); composed crouch today (0.0325, 0.2800, 0.4693); the same crouch under this rule (0, 0.0501, 0.4693); authored crouch (0, 0.0501, 0.5967).

Source of the authored horizontal components: the `pelvis.position` track of the authored clip where it has one (`vod7_crouch_idle`, `vod7_crouch_forward`, `vod7_jump_start`, `vod7_jump_land`; constant at (0, 0.0501) in the first two components). `vod7_jump_loop` has no `pelvis.position` track; for a clip without one the two components come from the pelvis bone's rest position, whose first two components are the same (0, 0.0501), checked in the GLB.

Guard limits. All limits of plan 07-27 stay, with one re-derivation stated here before any code is written: the smallest knee offset in crouch states was "at least 0.15 m" for the old leg shape (measured 0.172 and 0.179); with the pelvis over the feet the planning measurement gives 0.237 in crouch idle and 0.152 in crouch walk at 12 phases, and the plan check measured 0.137 over all 39 keys of the crouch walk, so the limit becomes at least 0.12 m (the authored crouch gives -0.246); the margin at full key resolution is 0.017 m. This is a limit for a changed composition, not a limit loosened after a failed run; it stays 0.12 and may not change again, whatever the built clips measure. For the jump family no smallest-knee limit exists; its per-key minimum is 0.003 m in jump start and jump land (0.014 and 0.005 at 12 phases), knees in front on every key. New limits: in every composed crouch and jump clip the pelvis horizontal position equals the authored one within 0.001 m at every key; the shoulder shift against composed standing is at most 0.001 m; in crouch idle the head bone and `spine_03` are within 0.02 m of their standing fore and aft positions. The ankle limit (within 0.03 m of the inherited ankle) stays; planning measured 0.009 once in jump land.
