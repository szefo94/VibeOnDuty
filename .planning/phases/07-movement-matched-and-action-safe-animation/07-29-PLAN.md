## The look-down assertion of `tests/first-person-rig.spec.js` (amendment after plan 07-28)

Plan 07-28 changed the body view offset to `-.18 - .1 * clamp(-pitch / 1.1)` (commit `779ef7f`); the eye is now in front of the chest at every pitch. Orchestrator decision, to be disclosed to the owner and judged by the review of plan 07-33: that offset stays. It is the fix for what the owner reported (H-13) and is not tuned to satisfy a test sample.

What the assertion is for. Lines 269 to 270 (added in plan 07-07, commit `b1e2801`) require, for the first-person samples at pitch -1.38, standing and crouched, that the body view's `calf_l`, `calf_r`, `foot_l` and `foot_r` (knee and ankle joints) project inside the screen and stand on the source's floor (`floorDelta` under 1e-6). They protect CONTEXT D-02, "torso and legs when looking down", and the shared floor of view body and world body. The loop of lines 226 to 258 takes the crouched sample at pitch -1.38 one 0.05 s tick after `crouching` first becomes true, in the middle of the inertia blend, when the pelvis position has already dropped and the leg rotations have not (feet 0.20 m below the root in the executor's replay).

The rule for this spec, in order:
1. The browser decides. If `tests/first-person-rig.spec.js` passes unedited, it is not edited.
2. If it fails, only this edit is allowed, and only if the sole failing expectation is the one on lines 269 to 270 and only for crouched samples:
   - Settling: inside the existing loop, when `crouching` is true and the pitch is -1.38 (the first crouched sample), the same `tickPresentation` call with the same observation is repeated, without sampling, until the source's pelvis height changes by less than 0.0001 m between two consecutive ticks, for at least 10 and at most 40 ticks of 0.05 s (the inertia blend ends after about 9 ticks). If it has not settled after 40 ticks the case fails. Nothing waits on wall time.
   - Assertion: the expectation is split by stance. For standing samples its predicate keeps the present text exactly (all four joints inside the screen, `floorDelta` under 1e-6). 3. Stop and return to the orchestrator with the measured coordinates, without editing, if in the browser after settling any of `calf_l`, `calf_r`, `foot_l` is not at least 0.1 inside the screen, or no leg joint at all is on screen, or a standing sample fails, or any other expectation of the file fails.
