Every turning waypoint is the centre of a one-cell lane, so the free half-width is `CELL / 2 - PLAYER_R` = 1.65 m. The values the helper exports and the tests re-derive:

- `WAYPOINTS`: `[[62,46],[62,22],[34,22],[34,46]]`, unchanged. Each is a cell centre of both lanes that meet there; no other point of those cells has more room, so moving a waypoint cannot gain margin.
- `LANE_MARGIN_M`: 0.40. `ARRIVAL_TOLERANCE_M`: 1.25 on each axis separately (a box, not a circle), which is `CELL / 2 - PLAYER_R - LANE_MARGIN_M`. A body anywhere in that box, radius included, stays 0.40 m inside its cell on both axes, so it is inside the next leg's lane whatever the stopping error of the previous leg was. The recorded failure position (35.76, 21.90) is outside this box on x and outside the leg 4 lane (limit 35.65), which is exactly the case the box excludes.
- Stopping: `STOP_LEAD_M` is 1.5 initially, the upper end of the measured stop step of about 1.2 to 1.5 m. Keys are released when the remaining distance along the driven axis to the waypoint centre is at most `STOP_LEAD_M`, so the expected stop lands at the centre with 1.25 m either way. If the median stop step measured by the tracer differs from 1.5 by more than 0.25 m, `STOP_LEAD_M` is set to that median and the SUMMARY states both values. `MAX_STOP_STEP_M` is `STOP_LEAD_M + ARRIVAL_TOLERANCE_M` (2.75): a stop that travels further than that after release has passed the far side of the box, and the control fails a run in which any single stop did so. At `CROUCH_SPEED` 2.75 m is 636 ms of stopping lag, more than two frames at 4.5 frames per second. A sample outside the lane stays the hard failure whatever the stop step was.
- `CROUCH_WITHIN_M`: 4. The last 4 m of a leg are walked crouched (4.32 m/s), as the existing controller already does within 2 m.
- `STALL_TICKS`: 150 server ticks (5 simulation seconds at 30 Hz) with a movement key held and less than `STALL_PROGRESS_M` 0.5 of progress along the track, the same rule as plan 07-23.
- `MATCH_SECONDS`: 600 (the operator default; `server/cli.js` allows 1 to 3600). `TIER_DEADLINE_MS`: 540000, `TIER_TEARDOWN_ALLOWANCE_MS`: 60000; their sum equals the pinned `onlineUnions` share of 600000 ms per tier in `COLLECTION_LIFETIME_PROJECTION` (`tools/art/animation-capture.mjs`, not edited). `LEG_BOUND_MS`: 90000, `ACTION_BOUND_MS`: 30000, `DEATH_BOUND_MS`: 60000, each cut short by whatever is left to the tier deadline.
- Control acceptance: tier wall time at most 360000 ms and `timeRemaining` at least 240 s when the tier completes. Basis: the slowest complete debug tier took 161020 ms at 3.9 to 5.3 frames per second, so 360000 ms leaves more than twice that and still 240 s under the tier allowance and the match limit.
